# Site chrome contract — 2026-08-24

Black family bar is the law on every public surface.

## Status / theme color
- PWA `manifest.json` `theme_color` is `#050505` (not gold).
- Page `<meta name="theme-color">` is `#050505` on Home, Launch, Chat, Forge, Shard, Contact, Privacy, Moments.
- Apple status bar: `black` (not a yellow overlay).
- Gold remains *inside* cards, buttons, and titles — never the system bar.

## Why this existed
Web-Forge on an installed Android PWA inherited `theme_color: #fcd34d` from the manifest and from Chat/Shard page metas. That painted a yellow strip above the black family nav.
