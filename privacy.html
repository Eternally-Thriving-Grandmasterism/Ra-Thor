Overview

Ra-Thor (rathor.ai) is designed with a fundamental commitment to user sovereignty and privacy. This website and all associated sovereign shards operate under strict principles of minimal data collection and maximum user control.

Autonomicity Games Inc. respects your privacy and operates under the TOLC 8 Mercy Gates.

Data Collection

This website collects no personal data whatsoever.

  • No cookies are used.
  • No tracking technologies or analytics are employed.
  • No personal information is requested, stored, or transmitted.
  • All computations and simulations run locally in your browser.
  • Lattice Chat stores sessions in IndexedDB / localStorage on this device only. Optional passphrase encryption uses Web Crypto (PBKDF2 + AES-GCM) in the browser. Nothing is uploaded to a Ra-Thor backend — we do not operate one.
  • Optional WebLLM and “Local Server” bridges stay on your machine. Connecting a local endpoint (Ollama / LM Studio) is your choice and does not send history to rathor.ai.

The commercial-inquiry form does not upload to rathor.ai; it opens your mail app.

How Ra-Thor Works

Ra-Thor is a mercy-gated symbolic lattice. When you use the connected demos (Grok or X integrations), those interactions are governed by the respective platforms’ (xAI / X Corp) privacy policies.

When you use Sovereign Shard v8 or Lattice Chat (workspace 14.15.6), the session store stays on your device. Optional encryption never leaves the browser. Bridging to Grok / X / another cloud model is explicit and then follows that provider’s policy.

Your Rights

Since no personal data is collected by this website, there is no personal data to access, correct, or delete on our servers. You retain full sovereignty over any local data:

  • Right of access — Inspect local chat/session data via Developer Tools → Application → IndexedDB / localStorage.
  • Right to erasure — Clear site data or delete IndexedDB at any time. Nothing is retained off-device.
  • Right to rectification — Edit or overwrite local session data in the chat interface or storage.
  • Right to data portability — Export sessions as JSON from the chat UI where available.
  • Right to object — Simply do not use optional chat features; the rest of the site requires zero data.
