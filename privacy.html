PRIVACY

Autonomicity Games Inc. respects your privacy and operates under the non-bypassable TOLC 8 Mercy Gates.

Ra-Thor™ collects no personal data beyond optional chat history stored locally in your browser using IndexedDB. This data remains exclusively on your device and is never transmitted to any server.

No tracking, no cookies, no third-party analytics, no fingerprinting, no advertising identifiers.

Data Subject Rights

Because all processing is local and offline-first, you already exercise full control:

  • Right of access — All data lives in your browser; inspect via Developer Tools → Application → IndexedDB.
  • Right to erasure (“right to be forgotten”) — Clear site data or delete the IndexedDB database at any time. Nothing is retained off-device.
  • Right to rectification — Edit or overwrite local session data directly in the chat interface or storage.
  • Right to data portability — Export any session as JSON from the chat UI (where available).
  • Right to object / restrict processing — Simply do not use the optional chat features; the rest of the site requires zero data.

Email contact (info@Rathor.ai), if used, is entirely optional and consent-based. Any correspondence is handled solely for the purpose you state and is never sold or shared.

We are committed to sovereign, offline-first design — your experience remains fully under your control. This policy is aligned with GDPR, CCPA, PIPEDA, and the EU AI Act principles of data minimisation and transparency.
