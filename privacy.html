Overview

Ra-Thor (rathor.ai) is designed with a fundamental commitment to user sovereignty and privacy. This website and all associated sovereign shards operate under strict principles of minimal data collection and maximum user control.

Autonomicity Games Inc. respects your privacy and operates under the TOLC 8 mercy checks.

Data Collection

The site itself collects no personal data: no sign-in, cookies, analytics or Ra-Thor server. Lattice Chat keeps your sessions in this browser’s localStorage on your device. If you choose an optional third-party service (opening Grok or X, Google Translate, downloading an on-device model, or a model server you configure), that service handles what you send under its own policy. If you buy a Ra-Thor Commercial Licence, see “Licence purchases” below.

  • No cookies are used.
  • No tracking technologies or analytics are employed.
  • The site itself requests, stores, and transmits no personal information.
  • All computations and simulations run locally in your browser.
  • Lattice Chat stores sessions in localStorage on this device only. Optional passphrase encryption uses Web Crypto (PBKDF2 + AES-GCM) in the browser. Living chat does not write IndexedDB. Nothing is uploaded to a Ra-Thor backend — we do not operate one.
  • Optional WebLLM and “Local Server” bridges stay on your machine. Connecting a local endpoint (Ollama / LM Studio) is your choice and does not send history to rathor.ai.

The commercial-inquiry form does not upload to rathor.ai; it opens your mail app.

Licence purchases

This section applies only if you buy a Ra-Thor Commercial Licence, by email or (when it opens) at online checkout. The site itself still collects no personal data.

  • Accountability. Autonomicity Games Inc. sells Ra-Thor commercial licences and is accountable for the personal information below. Our privacy contact is reachable at info@Rathor.ai.
  • What we collect. Only what we need to sell and manage a licence: your organisation’s name and billing address; your name and email; your tax ID if you give it; the licence tier you chose; your confirmation that you are buying for business purposes and are 18 or older and can bind the organisation; and your acceptance of the Terms of Sale.
  • Why. To form and manage your licence, send your receipt and a copy of the terms, handle refunds, prevent fraud, and keep tax and accounting records.
  • Consent. You consent to these uses by buying. We do not send marketing email without a separate opt-in, and we do not sell your information.
  • Payments. When online checkout is open, card payments are processed by Stripe on Stripe’s own pages. Your card details go to Stripe, not to us. Stripe’s privacy policy: stripe.com/privacy.
  • Where it is processed. Stripe and its service providers may store or process data outside Canada, for example in the United States. Information processed in another country may be accessible to that country’s courts, law enforcement and national security authorities.
  • How long we keep it. As long as tax and accounting law requires, then we delete it.
  • Safeguards. We protect purchase records with safeguards suited to their sensitivity, and share them only with the service providers named here and our professional advisers.
  • Access and correction. You can ask to see or correct your information by emailing info@Rathor.ai.
  • Questions and complaints. Email info@Rathor.ai. You can also complain to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada: priv.gc.ca.
  • Age. Licence purchases are for adults (18+) buying for a business.

Supporter Pass

This section applies only if you buy a Ra-Thor Supporter Pass, when it is offered. The site itself still collects no personal data. The Licence purchases points above on accountability, where data is processed, safeguards, access and correction, and questions and complaints apply to Supporter Pass records too.

  • What we collect. Stripe processes your payment on Stripe’s own pages, under Stripe’s own privacy policy: stripe.com/privacy. Your card details go to Stripe, not to us. We receive the purchase records Stripe provides: your name, email, country, the amount and the date.

Because the supporters page is kept in a public code repository, earlier versions in that repository’s history may still show a name after we remove it.

  • Age. Supporter Passes are for adults (18+).

How Ra-Thor Works

Ra-Thor is a mercy-checked symbolic lattice. When you use the connected demos (Grok or X integrations), those interactions are governed by the respective platforms’ (xAI / X Corp) privacy policies.

When you use the Shard demo or Lattice Chat, the session store stays on your device. Optional encryption never leaves the browser. Bridging to Grok / X / another cloud model is explicit and then follows that provider’s policy.

Your Rights

Browsing and chat data stay on your device, so there is nothing from browsing to access, correct, or delete on our servers. For licence purchases, see “Licence purchases” above. You retain full sovereignty over any local data:

  • Right of access — Inspect local chat/session data via Developer Tools → Application → Local Storage for this origin.
  • Right to erasure — Clear site data or delete this origin’s localStorage at any time. Browsing and chat data are not retained off-device. Licence purchase records are kept as described in “Licence purchases” above.
  • Right to rectification — Edit or overwrite local session data in the chat interface or storage.
  • Right to data portability — Export sessions as JSON from the chat UI where available.
  • Right to object — Simply do not use optional chat features; browsing the rest of the site requires zero data. Buying a licence requires the information described in “Licence purchases” above.
