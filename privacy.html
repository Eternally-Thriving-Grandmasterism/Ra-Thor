Overview

Ra-Thor (rathor.ai) is designed with a fundamental commitment to user sovereignty and privacy. This website and all associated sovereign shards operate under strict principles of minimal data collection and maximum user control.

Autonomicity Games Inc. respects your privacy and operates under the non-bypassable TOLC 8 Mercy Gates.

Data Collection

This website collects no personal data whatsoever.

  • • No cookies are used.
  • • No tracking technologies or analytics are employed.
  • • No personal information is requested, stored, or transmitted.
  • • All computations and simulations run locally in your browser.
  • • Optional Offline Lattice Chat history (if used) stays in IndexedDB on your device only — never uploaded.

How Ra-Thor Works

Ra-Thor is a mercy-gated symbolic AGI lattice. When you use the connected demos (Grok or X integrations), those interactions are governed by the respective platforms’ (xAI / X Corp) privacy policies.

When you use offline sovereign shards or the Offline Lattice Chat (available on this site and in the monorepo), all processing happens entirely on your device. No data leaves your machine.

Your Rights

Since no personal data is collected by this website, there is no personal data to access, correct, or delete on our servers. You retain full sovereignty over any local data:

  • Right of access — Inspect local chat/session data via Developer Tools → Application → IndexedDB / localStorage.
  • Right to erasure — Clear site data or delete IndexedDB at any time. Nothing is retained off-device.
  • Right to rectification — Edit or overwrite local session data in the chat interface or storage.
  • Right to data portability — Export sessions as JSON from the chat UI where available.
  • Right to object — Simply do not use optional chat features; the rest of the site requires zero data.

Compliance

Ra-Thor is designed to be compatible with:

  • • GDPR (General Data Protection Regulation)
  • • CCPA (California Consumer Privacy Act)
  • • COPPA (Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act)
  • • EU AI Act (GPAI obligations)
  • • Canadian IPC-OHRC Principles / PIPEDA-aligned minimisation

Because we collect no personal data, many compliance obligations are inherently minimized.

Third-Party Services

The landing page uses CDN resources (Tailwind CSS, Font Awesome) for presentation only. These do not track users in the context of Ra-Thor usage.

Connected demos on grok.com or x.com are subject to the privacy policies of xAI and X Corp respectively. Email to info@Rathor.ai is optional and consent-based; correspondence is never sold or shared.
