Licensing, access, and architecture

⚡Is Ra-Thor free to use?
Yes, for personal, educational, research, and professional assistance under AG-SML v1.1 — including freelancers and independent professionals earning modest income.
⚡When is a paid license required?
When the use is commercial, enterprise, or revenue-generating. The standard path is a time-boxed paid pilot, then a commercial license from Autonomicity Games Inc.
⚡Who qualifies for free use?
Any individual using Ra-Thor for personal needs, education, research, creative work, or daily assistance — including freelancers and independent professionals earning modest income.
⚡How does an organization license Ra-Thor?
Write to info@Rathor.ai with intended use, scale, and timeline. Request a time-boxed pilot or AG-SML commercial terms.
⚡What is Lattice Chat?
A private chat that runs on this device at /chat.html. No login. No keys we hold. Optional passphrase encryption.
⚡Are the science and aviation pages working plants or aircraft?
No. Sister public repositories under the same steward, license, and gates. Research surfaces — not plants, drugs, chains, ships, or certified aircraft.
⚡What are the PATSAGi Councils?
Permanent mercy-gated deliberation councils in the monorepo. Architecture and governance posture — not a warranty that every decision is automatically correct.
