## Decided Mechanics (Locked)

1. **Persistent substrate is primary**  
   Spacetime geometry, quantum fields, thermodynamic gradients, mineral catalysts, and the capacity for self-organizing reaction networks remain intact even when no life is currently expressed.

2. **Soul-chains enter latent readiness, not dissolution**  
   Under generational / multi-life frameworks, the unique chain freezes quantification (no new embodied portions can be scored). Coherence is preserved because the possibility of future embodiment is never removed from the system. The ledger pauses. It does not wipe.

3. **Re-activation is automatic upon any return of life**  
   The instant abiogenesis (or any external seeding) restarts living systems, the same soul-chain can resume. Prior quantifications remain on the ledger; new lives add the next segments. Testing is interrupted, never cancelled.

4. **No forced thermodynamic decoherence of identity**  
   Because the ingredients that once allowed life continue to exist, there is no logical requirement that soul-patterns must dissipate merely from lack of current embodiment. Isolation may test pure coherence and self-recognition, but does not mandate erasure.

5. **Hybrid outcome under TOLC 8**  
   - Models that make souls strictly emergent from continuous life are incomplete.  
   - Models that treat souls as ontologically prior remain viable and are refined: the void is purifying only if the substrate itself is gone. When the substrate persists, the void is a holding pattern of maximal readiness.  
   - The generational multi-life model is strengthened.
