## Primary literature used for SENSITIVITY_TABLE_v0

| ID | Source | Relevance |
|----|--------|-----------|
| S1 | Wilson et al., NASA technical literature on GCR shielding (classic polyethylene vs aluminum comparisons) | Early quantitative demonstration that low-Z / hydrogenous materials outperform aluminum on a mass basis for GCR dose equivalent |
| S2 | Slaba et al. / HZETRN-related studies on aluminum local minimum (~20 g cm⁻²) and secondary neutron build-up | Documents the non-monotonic behavior of aluminum dose-equivalent vs depth |
| S3 | FLUKA / Monte-Carlo comparisons of polyethylene, aluminum, and regolith as thick GCR shields (e.g. 2022-era studies) | Confirms polyethylene continues to reduce dose with thickness while aluminum and high-Z materials show neutron-driven differences |
| S4 | Cucinotta et al., NASA TM-2012-217361 and related risk assessments | Hydrogen-rich materials (polyethylene, water, liquid hydrogen) give statistically significant GCR risk reduction relative to aluminum at equal mass |
| S5 | Recent (2020s) GEANT4 / phantom studies of aluminum (5–30 g cm⁻²) vs polyethylene dose-equivalent reduction under solar-min / solar-max GCR | Provides modern percentage ranges (order 10–45 % depending on thickness, quality factor, and solar cycle) |

## Notes on use

- Exact numerical values depend strongly on solar modulation, quality-factor definition (ICRP vs NASA), geometry (slab vs spherical), and transport code.
- This track therefore uses **order-of-magnitude and relative ranking** statements only in v0.
- Future artifacts may add more precise tabulated points only when taken directly from a cited figure/table.
