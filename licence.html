Ra-Thor Commercial Licence

A plain summary for people and organizations who earn revenue with Ra-Thor or Rathor.ai.

Last updated: 2026-10-17 · Workspace 14.15.6

Who needs one

You need a Ra-Thor Commercial Licence if you are an organization using Ra-Thor or Rathor.ai, or if you sell it, a fork, a wrapper or a hosted instance, bundle it into a paid product, or run large-scale public multiplayer hosting of it for revenue or advertising, whichever AI model you run it with — for example Grok, Claude, ChatGPT, or others. Those names are examples only; no vendor is our partner, endorser, or affiliate. Use for personal, educational, research, or independent creative work stays free under AG-SML v1.1, even where modest personal income depends on the tool.

See LICENSE §2 for the full list.

When AG-SML may be enough

Personal use, education, research, and daily living or independent creative work by a natural person can stay under the public AG-SML v1.1 grant when it fits the licence files in the monorepo — even where modest personal income depends on the tool. The site FAQ states:

The Autonomicity Games Sovereign Mercy License. Individuals may use the work for personal, educational, research, and modest professional assistance. Commercial, enterprise, and revenue-generating use requires a paid license from Autonomicity Games Inc. License files live in the monorepo.

The repository LICENSE also says:

The following require a separate paid commercial license from Autonomicity Games Inc., written and signed (email to info@Rathor.ai):…

Licensor

Autonomicity Games Inc.

How to ask

Email info@Rathor.ai with your organization (if any), intended use, and timeline. This site does not publish prices, percentages, tiers, or checkout.

You may also use the commercial inquiry form on the contact page.
