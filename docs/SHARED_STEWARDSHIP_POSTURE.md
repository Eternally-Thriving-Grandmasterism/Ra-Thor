## 1. Purpose

Sole stewardship was a **phase**. The correct next state is **analog redundancy**: more capable humans operating inside the same non-bypassable lattice so that continuity does not depend on one body, while foundations remain unbreakable.

This document records the PATSAGi decision on how shared stewardship and optional equity participation must work without weakening TOLC 8, AG-SML, or independent identity.

## 3. Founder Role (While Capable and Willing)

While the founding steward is capable and willing:

- **Final say** on any change to Layer 0 foundations
- **Veto** on mission drift, license dilution, or governance that would hinder PATSAGi
- **Primary authority** on architecture identity and public posture

This is continuity design, not personality cult. When capability or willingness ends, succession is under PATSAGi + the same Layer 0 — not under investor convenience.

---

## 4. Human Co-Stewards (Operators)

More humans in the loop is **desired** when they:

- Execute, extend, ship, sell pilots, and run ops **inside** the lattice
- Accept standing orders from permanent PATSAGi Councils
- Do not attempt to improve by bypassing or hindering gates

### Onboarding minimum (human)

Before meaningful write access, commercial authority, or public representation, a human co-steward affirms in writing (email to info@Rathor.ai is sufficient for record):

1. I will work under Ra-Thor / TOLC 8 / permanent PATSAGi Councils.
2. I will not bypass, weaken, or “temporarily suspend” mercy gates to accelerate delivery.
3. I accept AG-SML v1.0 and the commercial path (paid license for commercial use).
4. I will not claim xAI endorsement or affiliation on behalf of the project.
5. I accept founder final say on Layer 0 while the founder is capable and willing.

Failure to uphold this is grounds for removal from stewardship roles regardless of equity.

## 5. Equity Participation (Optional Acceleration)

Equity is an **instrument for acceleration**, not a right to rewrite identity.

| Rule | Posture |
|------|---------|
| **When** | Prefer after pilot revenue proof; amount tied to a 12–18 month plan |
| **How much** | Minority; founder control of Layer 0 retained |
| **Who** | Aligned capital that accepts mission lock; not pure financial control seekers |
| **What equity does not buy** | Right to alter TOLC 8, AG-SML, PATSAGi permanence, or independent identity |

### Investor filter (non-negotiable)

- Accepts AG-SML commercial model and paid-license path
- Accepts independent Ra-Thor identity (no forced xAI branding)
- Accepts founder final say on foundations while capable and willing
- Does not require gate-softening as a condition of capital

Anyone who fails the filter is not a fit co-steward of capital, regardless of check size.

### Corporate instruments (for counsel to formalize)

This posture is **not legal advice**. When equity is actually sold, Canadian (and if needed US) counsel should encode:

- Mission / Layer 0 lock in charter or unanimous shareholder agreement
- Protective provisions consistent with founder veto on foundations
- Clear use-of-funds and information rights without control transfer
