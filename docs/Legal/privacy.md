## Data Collection

**The site itself collects no personal data: no sign-in, cookies, analytics or Ra-Thor server. Lattice Chat keeps your sessions in this browser’s localStorage on your device. If you choose an optional third-party service (opening Grok or X, Google Translate, downloading an on-device model, or a model server you configure), that service handles what you send under its own policy.** If you buy a Ra-Thor Commercial Licence, see “Licence purchases” below.

- No cookies are used.
- No tracking technologies or analytics are employed.
- The site itself requests, stores, and transmits no personal information.
- All computations and simulations run locally in your browser.
- Lattice Chat stores sessions in **localStorage** on the visitor’s device only. Optional passphrase encryption uses Web Crypto (PBKDF2 + AES-GCM) in the browser. Living chat does **not** write IndexedDB. Nothing is uploaded to a Ra-Thor backend — we do not operate one.
- Optional WebLLM and “Local Server” bridges stay on the visitor’s machine. Connecting a local endpoint (Ollama / LM Studio / OpenAI-compatible) is the visitor’s choice and does not send history to rathor.ai.
- **Sovereign Shard v8** is a local browser demo. It is not a live Lattice Conductor node.

This section applies only if you buy a Ra-Thor Commercial Licence, by email or at online checkout. The site itself still collects no personal data.

- **Accountability.** Autonomicity Games Inc. sells Ra-Thor commercial licences and is accountable for the personal information below. Our privacy contact is reachable at info@Rathor.ai.
- **What we collect.** Only what we need to sell and manage a licence: your organisation’s name and billing address; your name and email; your tax ID if you give it; the licence tier you chose; your confirmation that you are buying for business purposes and are 18 or older and can bind the organisation; and your acceptance of the Terms of Sale.
- **Why.** To form and manage your licence, send your receipt and a copy of the terms, handle refunds, prevent fraud, and keep tax and accounting records.
- **Consent.** You consent to these uses by buying. We do not send marketing email without a separate opt-in, and we do not sell your information.
- **Payments.** When online checkout is open, card payments are processed by Stripe on Stripe’s own pages. Your card details go to Stripe, not to us. Stripe’s privacy policy: https://stripe.com/privacy.
- **Where it is processed.** Stripe and its service providers may store or process data outside Canada, for example in the United States. Information processed in another country may be accessible to that country’s courts, law enforcement and national security authorities.
- **How long we keep it.** As long as tax and accounting law requires, then we delete it.
- **Safeguards.** We protect purchase records with safeguards suited to their sensitivity, and share them only with the service providers named here and our professional advisers.
- **Access and correction.** You can ask to see or correct your information, including your Supporter Pass records, by emailing info@Rathor.ai.
- **Questions and complaints.** Email info@Rathor.ai, including about your Supporter Pass records. You can also complain to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada: https://www.priv.gc.ca.
- **Age.** Licence purchases are for adults (18+) buying for a business.

## Supporter Pass

Supporter Passes are available to buy. This section applies only if you buy one. The site itself still collects no personal data. The Licence purchases points above on accountability, where data is processed, safeguards, access and correction, and questions and complaints apply to Supporter Pass records too.

- **What we collect.** Stripe processes your payment on Stripe’s own pages, under Stripe’s own privacy policy: https://stripe.com/privacy. Your card details go to Stripe, not to us. We receive the purchase records Stripe provides: your name, email, country, the amount and the date.
- **Why.** To deliver your pass, handle refunds, and keep tax and accounting records.
- **Optional display name.** At checkout you can type a name to show on the public supporters page, which is part of the public Ra-Thor repository and site. It does not have to be your real name. If you leave it blank, you are not listed. Nothing else about you is shown publicly: no email, country, amount or date.
- **Removing your name.** Email info@Rathor.ai at any time to have your name removed. We also remove it within 7 days of a refund or of the end of your pass. Because the supporters page is kept in a public code repository, earlier versions in that repository’s history may still show a name after we remove it.
- **Optional Discord username.** At checkout you can type your Discord username so we can give you the Supporter role on the AGi Discord server. It is optional. We use it only to give you the role and to remove it within 7 days of a refund or of the end of your pass. It is also kept with your Stripe purchase record, which we keep only as long as tax and accounting law requires. Discord handles your Discord account under its own privacy policy: https://discord.com/privacy.
- **No renewals, marketing or selling.** A pass does not renew, so we keep nothing for automatic renewal. We do not use Supporter Pass records for marketing, and we do not sell your information.
- **How long we keep it.** Purchase records: as long as tax and accounting law requires, then we delete them. Your display name: only while it is listed on the supporters page. A copy also stays with your Stripe purchase record, which we keep only as long as tax and accounting law requires. Your Discord username: in our own working list only until your role is removed; it also stays in the Stripe purchase record for as long as that record is kept.
- **Age.** Supporter Passes are for adults (18+).

## Your Rights

Browsing and chat data stay on your device, so there is nothing from browsing to access, correct, or delete on our servers. For licence purchases, see “Licence purchases” below. You retain full sovereignty over any local data:

- **Right of access** — Inspect local chat/session data via Developer Tools → Application → Local Storage for this origin.
- **Right to erasure** — Clear site data or delete this origin’s localStorage at any time. Browsing and chat data are not retained off-device. Licence purchase records are kept as described in “Licence purchases” above.
- **Right to rectification** — Edit or overwrite local session data in the chat interface or storage.
- **Right to data portability** — Export sessions as JSON from the chat UI where available.
- **Right to object** — Simply do not use optional chat features; browsing the rest of the site requires zero data. Buying a licence requires the information described in “Licence purchases” above.
