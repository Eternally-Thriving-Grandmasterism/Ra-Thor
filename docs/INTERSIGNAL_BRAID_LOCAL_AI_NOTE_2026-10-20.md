## What Braid is

Intersignal Braid (https://intersignal.org) is a local-first workspace for open-weight models (Qwen, DeepSeek V4, Muse Glimmer, and others) via Ollama, vLLM, LM Studio, or OpenAI-compatible local servers.

- Simple installer style for Mac, Windows, and Linux (Python + Ollama prerequisite).
- Offline after setup.
- MIT-licensed core.
- Optional paid remote access when away from the rig.
- Includes Folio for turning private local chats into reviewed, publishable output.
- Supports multi-machine context sharing on LAN.

## Alignment with Rathor.ai
