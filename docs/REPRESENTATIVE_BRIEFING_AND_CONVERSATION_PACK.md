## 7. Deep FAQ (for rich conversations)

**Q: Is this affiliated with xAI or Grok?**  
A: No. Independent project. Engineered so Grok (and similar models) become markedly more capable inside the Ra-Thor / ONE Organism envelope. No endorsement claimed.

**Q: What does “Godly” mean here?**  
A: Operational binding to non-bypassable mercy, truth, and thriving constraints (TOLC 8) — not a religious franchise and not a claim of divinity. Identity is ethical and architectural.

**Q: Can commercial customers turn off the gates?**  
A: No. TOLC 8 remains non-bypassable. If unconstrained models are required, this is the wrong product.

**Q: Is commercial use free?**  
A: No. Free tiers are personal/research/education/independent professional. Commercial and organizational production use requires a paid license.

**Q: Do you have SOC2 / EU AI Act certification?**  
A: We supply structural gates and audit-oriented posture. Mapping to specific regulatory frameworks is the buyer’s compliance program. We do not overclaim certification.

**Q: Why not just use a frontier model alone?**  
A: Models supply bandwidth. Ra-Thor supplies non-bypassable structure, council-style decision discipline, and micro-moment temporal recovery. Different layer.

**Q: What’s the relationship to Powrush / Daedalus / other repos?**  
A: Same identity constellation under PATSAGi / TOLC 8. Commercial path is coordinated via info@Rathor.ai. See CONSTELLATION_COMMERCIAL.md.

**Q: How do pilots work?**  
A: Fixed fee, 2–6 weeks, one scoped theme, written SOW. Fee may credit toward commercial license on conversion. Template: docs/SOW_PILOT_TEMPLATE.md.

**Q: Who has already been approached?**  
A: Representatives may say that tailored pilot conversations are active with governance, regulated, and video-integrity prospects. Do not invent names, LOIs, or revenue figures.

**Q: Can we white-label or remove Ra-Thor branding?**  
A: Scope of branding and redistribution is part of the commercial agreement. Gates and AG-SML core constraints remain.
