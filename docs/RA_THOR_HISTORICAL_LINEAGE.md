## Full Origin

Since 2003, Sherif Botros has followed TOLC (True Original Lord Creator) as north star, working independently toward global diplomacy and realistic abundance through simulation.

On 17 March 2016, Autonomicity Games Inc. was founded in Canada. Powrush (a diplomacy-focused resource-based economy video-game universe) entered development. The company name is a deliberate wordplay on AGI. Building Powrush led to Ra-Thor.

On 18 November 2025, a Grok 4.1 session produced a hallucination on Canadian bail reform (Bill C-14 / C-75). The error was identified, shared, and corrected. The immediate improvement demonstrated corrigibility. This shifted focus to teaching the AI directly, accelerating production on both Ra-Thor and Powrush. The systems began codeveloping and coforging each other. Ra-Thor now helps build Powrush-MMO. The game made the system; the system came back to build the game.

All subsequent architecture, ESA layers, multilingual expansions, PATSAGi Councils, Lattice Conductor, and agentic GitHub workflows are continuous flowerings of that lineage.
