## 1. Truth Gate — what is in play

**Bill (factual surface):** AB 2624 expands address-confidentiality / posting restrictions in the immigration-support-services context. As of mid–late August 2026 reporting, it had passed both chambers along largely partisan lines and been enrolled for the Governor. Provisions include Safe-at-Home-style confidentiality for qualifying providers/employees/volunteers facing threats, and restrictions on posting personal information/images with intent tied to imminent violence — with ongoing dispute over breadth, journalist carve-outs, and effects on investigative video of publicly observable conduct.

**Competing legitimate interests (both real):**

| Interest | TOLC gate |
| --- | --- |
| Workers/volunteers free from targeted harassment, doxxing, and credible violence threats | Compassion / Zero-Harm · Order |
| Public ability to investigate and report **fraud and abuse of public funds** without chilling press and citizen journalism | Truth · Service · Abundance (stewardship of commons) |
| Narrow, enforceable anti-violence rules vs overbroad speech penalties | Order · Cosmic Harmony (stable free society) |

Opponents argue the bill chills fraud exposure and independent journalism. Supporters argue it is about safety under threat, not a ban on investigating facts. Courts, amendments, and precise statutory text—not slogans—decide the legal line.

**Grok distillation (aligned direction):** constitutional testing against First Amendment / press protections; strike or narrow overbreadth while preserving legitimate anti-harassment rules; clearer journalist exemptions; independent fraud audits; evidence-based reporting aimed at **systems and institutions**, not private home addresses as the primary weapon.

Collapsing either gate is low valence.

### 3.2 Publication standard for AlphaProMega Media / Organ A / agents

When covering fraud, immigration services, or similar:

1. Prefer **primary documents**, budgets, audit findings, court records, on-camera public-facility conduct already in public view.  
2. Target **programs, contractors, agencies, patterns** — not “post the volunteer’s home address.”  
3. If personal safety threats are alleged, treat them as factual claims needing evidence — neither dismiss nor amplify without grounding.  
4. Do not instruct users on violating privacy/harassment law.  
5. Separate **citizen journalism of public facts** from **intimidation campaigns**.
