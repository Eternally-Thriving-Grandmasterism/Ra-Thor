## Core Machine

Collective power is a machine with **four load-bearing parts**:

1. **Capacity** — the ability to actually deliver security, adjudication, and the material conditions of common life.
2. **Purpose** — the justified ends: Order, Security, Justice, Flourishing & Welfare.
3. **Restraint** — hard moral and institutional limits on the means (violence, taxation, surveillance).
4. **Correction** — graduated, low-cost mechanisms that keep failure early and cheap (the Ladder of Graduated Corrigibility).

A polity (or collective intelligence) can fail at any one of them, and each failure has its own distinctive ruin.

**The only healthy attractor** is high Capacity under high Constraint (“Capable, Bounded, Corrigible Government”).
