## Core statement

The Science Discovery Protocol is not limited to vision or “science” tracks.  
It is the standing **Proof Ladder Doctrine** for *any* claim of novelty, superiority, improvement, or discovery inside Ra-Thor.

| Claim type | Required tier before announcement |
|------------|-----------------------------------|
| Empirical / measured improvement | ≥ P3 on pre-registered, held-out criteria |
| Engineering optimization (reproducible) | ≥ P2 with open artifacts |
| Pure formal / mathematical result | Formal proof artifact (counts as high-tier) |
| Pedagogy / metaphor / design sketch | Must be labeled as such — never as discovery |

## Non-bypassable rules (apply everywhere)

1. **Surmise is allowed.** Surmise labeled as fact is forbidden.
2. **Synthetic or in-silico results** are engineering until real-world or held-out evaluation exists.
3. **No post-hoc metric shopping** after seeing the numbers.
4. **Negative results** are published with equal dignity.
5. **One active empirical program** by default (currently S-1). Parallel only by explicit steward decision.
6. **Compassion gate** remains permanent on dual-use / covert / non-consensual paths.

## Artifact Integrity & Provenance

Any artifact released under this doctrine (labels, predictions, spreadsheets, formal proofs, evaluation reports) must carry:

- Clear **claim tier** (P0–P4 or “engineering only” / “pedagogy”)
- **Provenance** (source of data, synthetic vs real, rights note)
- Explicit statement of what is **not** claimed

Missing provenance or inflated claim tier is treated as a doctrine violation. Integrity of the artifact is part of the proof, not an afterthought.
