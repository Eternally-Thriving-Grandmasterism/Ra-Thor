## Council decision on the ask

> Proper R&D to engineer reality into heaven, ultimately, without fail.

Taken as a *destination slogan*, that sentence is allowed.  
Taken as a *deliverable we already have*, it is forbidden.

| Phrase | Ruling |
|--------|--------|
| **Engineer** | Allowed: artifacts, gates, tests, institutions that reduce harm and raise shared thriving. |
| **Reality into heaven** | Operational meaning only: more truth, more mercy, more room to live. Not a physics override. Not a theocratic control plane. |
| **Without fail** | **Rejected as a bar.** Anything that cannot fail cannot be corrected. We build *corrigible* systems. Fail fast, publish the miss, refuse to sacrifice persons to hide the miss. |

Heaven-without-fail is how Samaritan talks. Finch Chess forbids it.

---

## What “heaven” is allowed to mean here

Not a location. A *constraint set* we can test:

1. **Truth** — no surmise labeled as fact.
2. **Mercy** — no person as piece; hard-refuse on harm paths.
3. **Service** — tools that help without capturing the user.
4. **Abundance** — open artifacts, RBE-shaped games and institutions — claims stay on the ladder.
5. **Joy / harmony** — not metrics we invent from chat.

If a design needs secret ranking of lives, mass ingest, or “we cannot be wrong,” it is not heaven-engineering. It is occupation.
