## Who may use it for free?

A natural person, for personal, educational, research, or modest independent-professional work, under AG-SML v1.1 on the root `LICENSE`.

Clone the repo. Run it locally. Wrap a model *you* already pay for. No form.

"Modest" is undefined on purpose. If you are a one-person shop unsure which side you are on, write [info@Rathor.ai](mailto:info@Rathor.ai).

## Who must pay?

A paid written grant from Autonomicity Games Inc. is required for:

- org-wide or internal production use at scale
- a paid product, API, SaaS, store SKU, or consulting deliverable that wraps Ra-Thor / Rathor.ai
- revenue-linked or large-scale deployment
- selling copies, hosting for profit, or sublicensing for sale

If the lattice is part of how the organization ships or bills, it is not the free grant.

## What do they buy?

Rights and time. Not a token pack.

- Use of the named Work for the defined commercial purpose
- A human-replied pilot or commercial schedule
- Optional integration help on Enterprise / pilot paths

They keep their own model vendor keys. There is no public rathor.ai proxy and no instant license key.

## What do they not buy?

- ISO/IEC 42001, EU AI Act conformity, or METR numbers
- An AGSi warranty (Combined AGSi stays SURMISE)

- A checkout that turns Lattice Chat into a SaaS
- Custody, wallets, or an agent that places orders ([`NO_CUSTODY_NO_SIGN.md`](NO_CUSTODY_NO_SIGN.md))
- xAI affiliation

Outputs stay drafts until a human accepts them.

## Is this like those paid wraps that sit on Stripe?

No.

Those shops sell inference. We sell a constitution and inspectable software. A public "pay us, we wrap Grok" app would make us the model vendor. That is outside this grant and outside [`NO_CUSTODY_NO_SIGN.md`](NO_CUSTODY_NO_SIGN.md).

A named organization pilot may sit the wrap on *their* keys or *their* VPC. That is a service conversation, not a repo feature and not a public SKU.

## How do I start?

1. [Commercial inquiry form](https://rathor.ai/contact.html#commercial-inquiry), or email **info@Rathor.ai**.
2. Say: organization name, intended use, approximate scale, and whether you want a **pilot**, an **independent evaluation**, or a **full commercial license** talk.
3. A human replies (target 1–2 business days for a serious note).
4. Agreement → invoice → activation.

There is no public annual price list. Fees and any royalty rate live only in the signed schedule.

Pilot envelopes that *are* public: [`PILOT_EVALUATION_BANDS.md`](PILOT_EVALUATION_BANDS.md).
