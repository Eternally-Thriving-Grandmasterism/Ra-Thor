## Intention of the wrap

A human brings a model. Ra-Thor is the **envelope**, not the model.

1. Layer 0 (TOLC 8) cannot be voted off, prompted off, or hot-swapped away.
2. PATSAGi Councils deliberate **under** the gates. A majority cannot turn Rejected into Apply.
3. Lattice Conductor v14 sequences. It does not replace gates or councils.
4. The model is optional neural bandwidth inside ONE Organism. It is not the sovereign.
5. Human override stays on every research draft and every public claim.
6. Offline-first: Lattice Chat / shards may run with no Ra-Thor backend.
7. Fail closed. If valence or evidence is missing, refuse or refine. Do not invent scores.
