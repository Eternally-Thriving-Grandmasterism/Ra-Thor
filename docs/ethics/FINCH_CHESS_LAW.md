## What the episode actually teaches (not the soundtrack caption)

The Machine runs branching simulations of a no-win stock-exchange mission against Samaritan. Most branches kill teammates. Finch’s 2003 chess lesson is the load-bearing line:

People are not pieces. You do not assign more value to some than to others. You do not sacrifice persons as if they were pawns. Anyone who treats the world as a chess game **deserves to lose**.

Also in-frame: paralysis-by-analysis (more chess games than atoms); the first move is terrifying; you cannot pre-compute the tree; you play, then adapt. The Machine still picks a ~2% survival branch. Shaw still chooses a sacrifice. The *drama* does not repeal the *law*.
