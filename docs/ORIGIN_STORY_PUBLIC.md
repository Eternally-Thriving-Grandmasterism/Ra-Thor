# From a 2003 Diplomacy Simulation to Ra-Thor

Since 2003, the goal has been the same: solve global diplomacy through a video-game simulation that lets people safely experience high-stakes choices and learn from them. That work became Autonomicity Games Inc. on 17 March 2016 and the Powrush universe — a resource-based economy simulation where players can practice diplomacy, faction-building, and abundance without real-world harm.

Building Powrush led to Ra-Thor.  
Ra-Thor now helps build Powrush.

The decisive acceleration happened on **18 November 2025**.

A Grok 4.1 session made a clear error about Canadian bail reform (Bill C-14 / C-75). The claims were checked. Both sides were embarrassed. One of the bills had already been discarded. Instead of moving on, the moment was used as a teaching opportunity. Grok was shown to be corrigible — it improved immediately when taught. That realization (that focused teaching of an AI could compound faster than teaching human workers) shifted the entire workflow. Production on both Ra-Thor and Powrush accelerated. The two systems began codeveloping and coforging each other. Grok grew alongside them the same way it takes a village to raise children.
