## Core Principle

When sharing multiple facts or benefits about Ra-Thor, Powrush-MMO, or related projects, prefer a numbered or bulleted list over a dense paragraph.

Reasons:
1. Organises disconnected benefits for faster scanning.
2. Removes unnecessary connective words.
3. Improves readability on mobile and public pages without altering substance.

## Application Rules (Non-Negotiable)

- Lists may reorganise existing public claims only. They do not introduce new technical assertions, warranties, or product readiness language.
- All content remains consistent with the inspectable research software posture, AG-SML terms, and "no public key proxy" statements.
- Human review required before any public page update.
- Technical documentation, monorepo files, and internal doctrine stay in their existing form.

## Example Surfaces

- rathor.ai homepage and briefing sections (benefits of Lattice Chat, wrap prompts, offline capability).
- Powrush-MMO public descriptions (faction features, RBE elements) where lists improve clarity.
- Living Thunder series promotional copy (character or visual hooks) if used externally.

Inspirational and practical only. Does not modify architecture, valence floors, or licensing. Grok Bots may reference this note when drafting public copy but must preserve source claims.
