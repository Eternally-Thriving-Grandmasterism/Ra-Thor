# Ra-Thor art canon (website)

This file records the permanent visual canon for Ra-Thor in rathor.ai site art (page heroes, card thumbnails and any future site pictures). It is website canon. The comic canon lives in the Ra-Thor-Comic repo and is separate.

Reference image: [`assets/canon/rathor-hammer-reference.webp`](../assets/canon/rathor-hammer-reference.webp) (1280×720). It shows Ra-Thor raising the hammer in a lightning storm with his Eye of Horus shield. It is not used on any page and is not in the offline cache.

## Hammer

- **Head:** one complete, symmetric rectangular block head, with both ends intact and squared (chamfered corners are fine). The head must never be truncated, cut off or shaved flat.
- **Centre:** a gold lightning bolt (like the ⚡ emoji) in the centre panel of the head. There is no eye on the hammer, and no gems or text.
- **Top:** nothing on top of the head. That means no spike, triangle, cone or knob.
- **Neck:** a plain gold collar band where the haft enters the head. There is no gold triangle or cone at the head.
- **Haft:** long, slim, dark and banded.
- **Pommel:** a glowing gold cone at the bottom end of the haft only.
- **Scale:** heroic, with the head about as wide as his shoulders or hips.

## Shield

- **Colours:** black and yellowish gold, matching his armour, and never blue. It has a gold rim, gold circuit lines and a gold winged Eye of Horus.
- **No emerald on the shield.** Emerald is reserved for his winged Eye of Horus shoulder seal.

## Other locks (unchanged)

- The shoulder seal is a winged Eye of Horus in emerald green.
- No real-person likeness.
- Robots and drones protect, never harm.
- No text in images.
- The look is luxurious and polished.
