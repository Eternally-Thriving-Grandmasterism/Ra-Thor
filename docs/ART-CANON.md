# Ra-Thor art canon (website)

This file records the permanent visual canon for Ra-Thor in rathor.ai site art (page heroes, card thumbnails and any future site pictures). It is website canon, separate from the comics; per Sherif, the comics may copy it later if it is easy to reproduce. The comic canon lives in the Ra-Thor-Comic repo.

Reference image: [`assets/canon/rathor-hammer-reference.webp`](../assets/canon/rathor-hammer-reference.webp). It was supplied by Sherif in chat at 2:17 PM ET Nov 25, and resized to 1280×720. It shows Ra-Thor raising the hammer in a lightning storm with his Eye of Horus shield. No page uses it and it is not in the offline cache.

Labels: **Ruling** means a Sherif quote below directly supports the item ("via the model" means the 3:00 PM ruling, through what the chat.html and pilot.html hammers show). **Inference (Wild), not a Sherif ruling** means it comes from the reference image, an earlier brief, or Wild's reading, and has not been ruled on in words.

## Hammer

**Model (Ruling, 3:00 PM ET Nov 25):** the hammers in the chat.html hero (`assets/art/hero-chat-library-counsel-*.webp`) and the pilot.html hero (`assets/art/hero-pilot-scoped-workbench-*.webp`, the hammer on the table) are the model for every hero. Where the 2:17 PM reference image and these two hammers differ, the two model hammers win.

**Examples (Ruling, 8:30 PM ET Nov 25):** his three good examples are the whitepaper hero (the docs cover picture of Ra-Thor reading a book, no hammer), the employ.html hero (`assets/art/hero-employ-commission-hall-*.webp`, the hammer standing head-down) and the pilot.html hero (the hammer on the table). The employ.html and pilot.html hammers set the detailing: bevelled edges, carved relief, the framed bolt panel, the collar and the haft. For the outline of the head, the 8:47 PM ruling keeps the triangular-hump shape canon (see Ends).

- **Head (Ruling, 2:15 PM, 3:00 PM and 8:47 PM):** one complete, symmetric block head of dark bronze-iron, with both ends intact, bevelled edges and carved knotwork relief, and hump-shaped ends (see Ends). It must never be truncated, cut off or shaved flat.
- **Ends (Ruling, 8:00 PM and 8:47 PM):** each end of the head has the triangular-hump shape of the chat.html hammer head (pointed hump ends), never a plain rectangle. At 8:47 PM, on the Forge card thumbnail, Sherif wrote: "the hammer is still rectangular instead of becoming the strange iconic shape with the triangular hump design we made canon." Earlier, at 8:30 PM, he answered a question card ("Which part of my before-and-after is off canon?") by picking the option "Both hammers and eyes", after typing "This is off canon entirely. Use these good examples." The 8:47 PM words settle that the hump shape stays canon.
- **Open exception, awaiting Sherif:** at 8:47 PM he called the examples hero proof row okay. That hammer has a squared block head with the employ.html bolt panel and no humps, so it keeps that head for now.
- **Centre (Ruling, 3:00 PM and 8:30 PM, via the examples):** a recessed dark hexagonal or elongated-octagon panel framed in gold, holding a glowing gold lightning bolt (like the ⚡ emoji). There is no eye, gem or text on the hammer.
- **Top (Ruling, 1:22 PM, 1:43 PM and 3:00 PM):** nothing on top of the head, so no cone, triangle or spike.
- **Neck (Ruling, 3:00 PM, via the model):** a round gold boss or stud where the haft enters the head, with gold ring bands below it, as on the model hammers. There is no gold triangle or cone at the head.
- **Haft (Ruling, 1:32 PM and 3:00 PM):** the hammer always has its handle. It is long, slim, dark, cross-hatched (wrapped) and has thin gold bands, as on the model hammers.
- **Pommel (Ruling, 3:00 PM, via the model):** a gold cone at the bottom end of the haft only.
- **Scale:** heroic, with the head about as wide as his shoulders or hips. *Inference (Wild), not a Sherif ruling.* The two model hammers are shown at different distances and do not settle the scale.
- **Which faces carry the bolt:** *unruled.* The model hammers show the bolt on the one visible face.

## Shoulder eye (Rulings, 8:30 PM and 8:47 PM)

Scope: this rule covers the three reference heroes (whitepaper, employ.html, pilot.html) and the pictures repainted in EYES-CANON-3 (the Launch, Shard and Forge card thumbnails, and the chat.html hero and its card thumbnail). It is not yet a rule for every picture.

- One large Eye of Horus in bright, glowing emerald-green line art (brow, eye outline, round pupil, teardrop line and spiral tail) sits centred on the far pauldron (his left, the shield side). It is about half the pauldron's width, on black-and-gold armour, sometimes with a dark green tint on the plate.
- **Near pauldron (Ruling, 8:47 PM):** where a picture had a good near-shoulder eye, it keeps one as a clear, correct Eye of Horus in the same style, and it must not be removed. The Launch card thumbnail and the chat.html hero (with its card thumbnail) carry a clear eye on both shoulders. The Shard and Forge card thumbnails, which he called okay, keep the far eye only. In the whitepaper and pilot heroes the near pauldron shows no eye, and in the employ hero only a sliver shows.
- **Glyph (Ruling, 9:08 PM):** Sherif wrote: "Need the opposite for the eye without the excessive lines and no double bottom curves." That means no excessive lines and no double bottom curves.
  - *Lead's reading of "the opposite":* near-shoulder eyes are drawn mirrored, facing the opposite way to the far-shoulder eye. This is an inference, not his words.
  - *Lead's inference on anatomy:* the glyph copies the whitepaper.html hero's shoulder eye: one brow, one eye outline with a round pupil, one teardrop line and one spiral curl at the bottom. These details go beyond his words.
- **Open exception, kept as shipped:** the chat.html hero's far-shoulder eye is the original from main and still curls on both sides at the bottom, so it does not meet the Glyph rule. It stays as is because on 2026-11-26 Sherif chose to ship this version (card answer: picked option "Put this version live once the councils vote").
- **Open exception, awaiting Sherif:** the examples.html hero (unchanged in EYES-CANON-3) still shows a large eye on his right pauldron. It is the standing picture Sherif sent at 6:01 PM as a good-eyes example (ref-1). Whether that placement stays, or follows the far-pauldron rule, is his call.

## Two suit styles (Ruling, 8:30 PM)

Ra-Thor has two personified suits that are thematically similar, like Batman wearing different suits. Sherif, verbatim: "I'm aware we have 2 personified styles active for Ra-Thor like Batman wearing different yet thematically similar suits, Mate". Both keep the black-and-gold armour, the winged helmet with a halo, the green Eye of Horus on the shoulder and the lightning-bolt hammer. For site heroes, his three examples above set which details to copy.

## Shield

- **Colours (Ruling, 12:43 PM and 1:06 PM):** black and yellowish gold, like the armour, and never blue.
- **Gold rim, gold circuit lines and an Eye of Horus:** *Inference (Wild), not a Sherif ruling*, from the 2:17 PM example and the existing heroes.
- **Wings on the Eye:** *unruled.* The reference shield's Eye has no wings, while the shipped heroes have a winged Eye.
- **No emerald on the shield:** *Inference (Wild), not a Sherif ruling.* It comes from the earlier ART-UPLIFT-3 brief (Nov 23), and no Sherif quote is on file here.

## Other locks

None of these has a Sherif quote on file in this record. Each comes from the ART-UPLIFT-3 card brief (Lead Mate, Nov 23 2026), and each is *Inference (Wild), not a Sherif ruling*:

- The shoulder seal is an Eye of Horus in emerald green. Its size and placement are now ruled (see Shoulder eye).
- No real-person likeness.
- Robots and drones protect, never harm.
- No text in images.
- The look is luxurious and polished.
