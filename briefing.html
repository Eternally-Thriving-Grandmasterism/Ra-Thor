The employ loop

  1. 1. Intend — ask for a draft, a plan, a review, or a refusal.

Third paths, not coerced binaries

Trolley problems force “hurt A or hurt B.” TOLC 8 is written so the first move is not “pick a victim.” Truth asks whether the frame is honest. Order asks whether the situation can be slowed or rerouted. Compassion is zero-harm as the standing test.

A third path is the refusal to treat a coerced binary as the whole map. That is design intent — not a certificate that every session yields a miracle. Humans still review.
